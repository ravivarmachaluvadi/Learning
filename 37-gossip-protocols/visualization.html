Rumour Mill

Twenty machines need to agree on two things: one shared fact, and which of them are still alive. No machine is in charge, none of them can shout to everyone, and messages get lost. Each one does the only thing it can — every second, it picks a couple of others at random and tells them what it knows. Watch what that is enough for, and where it isn't.

A round here is one tick in which every node gossips once with what it knew when the round began. Real gossip is asynchronous and a "round" is just each node's own timer firing — the shape of the spread, and every failure on this page, are the same either way.
