Outbox Crash Lab

A shop must do two things for every order: write it in its own ledger (the database) and tell the warehouse (send a message). This page lets you crash the shop at the worst possible instants and watch what each strategy loses.

The setup. Stock of red mugs starts at 10. Every order is for 2 mugs, so after one order the warehouse's count should say 8. The middle three stations exist because the shop's database and the warehouse cannot share one transaction — something must carry the news between them.

New here? Press “Run the whole story for me” — it plays four short acts with narration: the naive strategy losing an order, the outbox surviving the same crash, the outbox's one honest weakness (duplicates), and the fix.
