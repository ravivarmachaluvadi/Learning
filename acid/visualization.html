Vikram's Morning

A branch office keeps one bound ledger — Ravi's balance on page 5, Meera's on page 9, ₹1000 between them. Asha the cashier moves money by changing two numbers. At any instant the fire alarm can empty the building, and next morning a different cashier, Vikram, must put the books right knowing only what survived the night. Everything here is a database: Asha's desk is memory, the safe is the disk, and the rough day-book is a write-ahead log. Break it, then watch recovery fix it.
