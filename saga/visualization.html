Saga Trip Desk

A travel agency books a trip — flight, then hotel, then car — across three companies that share nothing. No single transaction can cover all three. Watch a saga book them one commit at a time, and undo them backwards when a step fails.

The setup. Each company has 1 seat / room / car left. A trip needs all three: half a trip is worse than none. The coordinator keeps a durable log of what's done — its only memory if it crashes.

New here? Press “Run the whole story for me”: four narrated acts — a smooth booking, a failure with the undo cascade, a coordinator crash and recovery, and the same saga with no coordinator at all.

Who coordinates the trip?
Orchestration: one place knows the plan and keeps the saga log.
What goes wrong on this trip?
Failures strike the booking (or cancellation) attempt itself.
Step or crash — your call
“Next step” runs ONE action at a time. Crash the coordinator between any two actions and see what its log does and doesn't remember.
