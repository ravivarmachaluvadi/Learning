What this model does and does not know

It knows: task durations vary and are right-skewed; interrupts remove capacity; new joiners ramp up over about six weeks and cost a mentor a quarter of a day; every engineer costs the others a little coordination; sustained overtime buys hours and spends energy and quality; bugs are created while coding and discovered weeks later, then jump the queue.

It does not know: dependencies between tasks (real critical paths are worse than this), people quitting, morale, a task that turns out to be impossible, or the one senior engineer everything routes through. The overtime and ramp-up curves have the shape published studies report, but the exact constants are this model's, not a measurement of your team. Treat every number as the direction and rough size of an effect, not a prediction.
