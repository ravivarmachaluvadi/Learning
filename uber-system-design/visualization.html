Dispatch Console

Somebody standing on a street corner taps a button. Twelve cars are moving around this five-kilometre square. In the next few hundred milliseconds the system has to pick one of them, and make sure nobody else is promised the same car. Everything below is that decision, slowed down so you can watch it — and every rule it follows is a switch you can flip.
