Merge Without a Referee

Three phones hold the same shopping list. They edit it while offline, then sync in whatever order the network feels like — dropping messages, delivering some twice, and cutting the group in half for a while. Nobody is in charge. Watch which designs survive that, and which quietly throw someone's work away.
