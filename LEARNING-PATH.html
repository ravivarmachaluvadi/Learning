How this order was derived, and where to distrust it

The data is real. Every prerequisite link, date, and count on this page is parsed from the Builds on lines in your own KNOWLEDGE-MAP.md — 478 links across 71 labs. 476 of them name another lab by its exact folder name and are drawn; the other two ([uber] under idempotency, [whatsapp] under idempotency) match no lab and are not. The graph has thirty-eight cycles (for example cqrs → idempotency → cqrs), so no order satisfies every link; the stages break the ties.

But a plain topological sort would be useless here, and this page does not use one. Those links were written at learning time, so they can only ever point at labs you had already finished — sorting by them just replays your calendar. The eight stages are a curriculum judgement laid over the graph, written down in LEARNING-PATH.md: group by what the ideas are about, then order the groups so nothing needs a word it has not met.

What that means for you. Stage membership is arguable — tls and ssh could sit in stage 1, and idempotency genuinely belongs in three places at once. The counts, dates, and prerequisite chips are not arguable; those are measured. When the two disagree, trust the counts.
