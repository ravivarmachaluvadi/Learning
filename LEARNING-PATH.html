How this order was derived, and where to distrust it

The data is real. Every prerequisite link, date, and count on this page is parsed from the Builds on / Related / Next lines in your own KNOWLEDGE-MAP.md — 398 links across 62 labs. The dependency graph is acyclic, so a valid order exists.

But a plain topological sort would be useless here, and this page does not use one. Those links were written at learning time, so they can only ever point at labs you had already finished — sorting by them just replays your calendar. The seven stages are a curriculum judgement laid over the graph, not an output of it: group by what the ideas are about, then order the groups so nothing needs a word it has not met.

What that means for you. Stage membership is arguable — tls and ssh could sit in stage 1, and idempotency genuinely belongs in three places at once. The counts, dates, and prerequisite chips are not arguable; those are measured. When the two disagree, trust the counts.
