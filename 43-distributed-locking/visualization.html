The Spare Key

A photography club has one darkroom and one key. If a keyholder goes silent, the front desk hands out a numbered spare after a timeout — which rescues crashes and creates a monster: a keyholder who was only asleep. Watch clients acquire a lock, stall, crash, and write late — and watch the enlarger's token dial become the only thing standing between a nap and corrupted data. Time moves in ticks; nothing is random.
