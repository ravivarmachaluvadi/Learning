Rupaya's settlement service reads three captured payments, writes ledger lines and payout rows, and records its progress — five appends across three partitions that must all count or none of them. Step through the protocol one message at a time and watch the one thing that surprises everybody: the records appear in the log long before any decision exists, and an abort never removes them.

A teaching model of Kafka's transaction protocol, not an implementation. Offsets, states and message names follow KIP-98, KIP-447 and KIP-890 and the Kafka 4.3 documentation; timing, batching, replication and the aborted-transaction index are simplified away. Defaults shown (transaction.timeout.ms=60000, isolation.level=read_uncommitted) are the real 4.3 defaults.
