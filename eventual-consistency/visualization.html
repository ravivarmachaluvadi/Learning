The Weakest Promise

The village of Deodar keeps its news on three noticeboards. Anyone may post or read at any board, instantly. Couriers carry copies between the squares. The parish council printed exactly one promise about the boards — and a panel of four judges below watches every read and rules on whether any promise, made or merely assumed, has been broken.

The council promises

“If everyone stops posting, the boards will eventually all show the same notices.”

We do not say when a notice arrives, what you will see in the meantime, which of two quarrelling notices survives, or what happens if you walk from one square to another. — This is eventual consistency, in full.
