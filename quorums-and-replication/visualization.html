Why writing to two of three is enough

Three machines each keep a copy of the same value. You do not have to reach all of them to write, and you do not have to ask all of them to read — but if the group you write to and the group you read from can miss each other, you will read something that is out of date and nothing will warn you. Move the two sliders and watch the arithmetic decide.
