The Append-Only Bank

This bank stores no balance anywhere. It keeps a passbook — an append-only list of facts — and computes any balance, at any moment in history, by reading the book up to that line. Deposit and withdraw, drag the time slider to revisit any past version, race two tellers against one account, post a mistake and correct it in ink. The stories on the left narrate the scenes worth seeing.

Rules this page uses: every command is decided against the state replayed from the book, then appended only if the book still ends at the version the decider read (“expected version”) — that check is what the tellers exercise. Withdrawals may not overdraw. A teller “holding the book” means they loaded some version and will write against it; in real systems the two tellers are two app servers. The slider never changes the book — time travel is a read, and that it is always possible is the pattern's gift.
